    Cyber Safety Tips

    Protect yourself with these essential cybersecurity practices

    Check for HTTPS

    Always look for the padlock icon and "https://" in the URL bar. Legitimate sites encrypt your data.

    Verify Domain Names

    Scammers use misspellings like "paytm-kyc.xyz" instead of "paytm.com". Always double-check.

    Ignore Urgency

    Scam messages create false urgency — "Act NOW", "Account BLOCKED". Real companies don't pressure you.

    Never Share OTPs

    No legitimate bank or service will ask for your OTP, PIN, or password via call, SMS, or email.

    Avoid Shortened URLs

    Shortened links (bit.ly, tinyurl) hide the real destination. Hover or expand them before clicking.
